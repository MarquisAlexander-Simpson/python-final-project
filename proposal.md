## What will (likely) be the title of your project?

Basketball Shoutout

## In just a sentence or two, summarize your project. (E.g., "A website that lets you buy and sell stocks.")

My project will be a two-player basketball shooting game where both players compete in a three-point contest. Each player will take turns shooting and try to score more points than their opponent

## In a paragraph or more, detail your project. What will your software do? What features will it have? How will it be executed?

I plan to create a graphical basketball game using Python. The game will allow players to take shots from behind the three-point line and try to score as many points as possible. It will include a basketball court, a hoop, a basketball, and a scoreboard. Players will be able to use left and right controls to adjust their shooting angle. They will also hold a button to build up shot power and release it to shoot. The game will keep track of the player's points and the number of shots remaining. I also want to include Easy, Medium, and Hard difficulty levels that change how challenging it is to make a shot. Once the player runs out of shots, the game will display their final score. The game will be played on a computer using a graphical interface.
